ADA is the all-in-one city guide for residents, students and newcomers in North Cyprus (TRNC / KKTC). Everything you need in Nicosia (Lefkoşa), Kyrenia (Girne), Famagusta (Gazimağusa), Güzelyurt, İskele and Lefke, in one app.

- Explore map – places, cafés, restaurants, beaches and city walking routes
- Events – concerts, festivals, theatre and ticket info
- Property & stays – homes for sale, rentals, short stays and student dorms
- Repairs & maintenance – trusted tradespeople and companies
- Pets – vets and pet hotels

Available in 9 languages, including English, Turkish, Russian, Arabic and Persian. Whether you've lived here for years or just arrived for university, ADA makes everyday life in Northern Cyprus easier.
