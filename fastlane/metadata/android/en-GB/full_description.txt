ADA is the all-in-one city guide and assistant for residents, students and newcomers in North Cyprus (TRNC / KKTC). Everything you need in Nicosia (Lefkoşa), Kyrenia (Girne), Famagusta (Gazimağusa), Güzelyurt, İskele and Lefke, in one app.

- Explore map – places, cafés, restaurants, beaches and city walking routes
- Events – concerts, festivals, theatre and ticket info
- Property & stays – homes for sale, rentals, short stays and student dorms
- Hotels – hotels across North Cyprus with their contact details
- Food & drink – browse menus, build your basket and order
- Announcements – exams, job vacancies, scholarships and tenders, each linked to its official source
- Student Hub – universities, student portals and the basics of student life
- Mobile line – choose your eSIM, line and package before you land in Cyprus
- Exchange rates – Central Bank of the TRNC rates and a currency calculator
- Check-in – check in at places and see what's happening around the city
- Oli – ask a question and Oli answers from ADA's own information
- Towing & roadside assistance – companies serving your area
- Driving licence – driving school branches and licence classes
- Repairs & maintenance – trusted tradespeople and companies
- Pets – vets and pet hotels

Available in 9 languages, including English, Turkish, Russian, Arabic and Persian. Whether you've lived here for years or just arrived for university, ADA makes everyday life in Northern Cyprus easier.

INFORMATION SOURCES

ADA displays publicly available information published by the organisations below. ADA is not the origin of this information. Please confirm details with the official source before acting on them.

Exchange rates — Central Bank of the TRNC: https://mb.gov.ct.tr/tr/veriler/doviz_kurlari/kur_sorgulama

Embassies and consular information — TRNC Ministry of Foreign Affairs: https://mfa.gov.ct.tr/tr

Border crossings, driving rules and traffic information — TRNC Police General Directorate: https://www.polis.gov.ct.tr/

Public-service exams and job vacancies — TRNC Public Service Commission: https://khk.gov.ct.tr/

Education announcements — TRNC Ministry of National Education: https://www.mebnet.net/

Higher education announcements — TRNC Higher Education Council (YÖDAK): https://yodak.gov.ct.tr/

Public tenders — TRNC Ministry of Finance: https://maliye.gov.ct.tr/

Inside the app, every listing taken from an official source links back to that source.
