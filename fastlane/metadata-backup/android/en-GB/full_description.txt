ADA is your all-in-one assistant for North Cyprus (TRNC) — built for newcomers, residents, and visitors who want everything about the island in one place.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH ADA:

Discover
Beaches, castles, landmarks, and local events happening across the island.

Getting around
Bus routes, taxis, car rentals and airport transfers across TRNC.

Home & living
Home services, accommodation, and estate listings.

Newcomer essentials
Border crossings, embassies, driving info, currency, and exchange rates.

Pets & animals
Vet listings and pet care information.

Local directory
Businesses and services near you — address, phone and opening hours.

Available in 9 languages: English, Turkish, Arabic, Russian, Greek, French, Spanish, German, and Farsi.

INFORMATION SOURCES

ADA displays publicly available information published by the organisations below. ADA is not the origin of this information. Please confirm details with the official source before acting on them.

Exchange rates — Central Bank of the TRNC: https://mb.gov.ct.tr/tr/veriler/doviz_kurlari/kur_sorgulama

Embassies and consular information — TRNC Ministry of Foreign Affairs: https://mfa.gov.ct.tr/tr

Border crossings, driving rules and traffic information — TRNC Police General Directorate: https://www.polis.gov.ct.tr/

Inside the app, every listing taken from an official source links back to that source.
