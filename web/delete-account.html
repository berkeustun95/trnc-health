ADA Account deletion

Delete your ADA account

You can delete your ADA account, and the personal data linked to it, at any time. You can also remove some of your data without deleting your account.

Delete your account in the app

Recommended — takes effect immediately

Open ADA, go to Profile, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm. If you signed in with Google or Apple, ADA also disconnects itself from your Google or Apple account.

Or ask us by email

✉️

Account Deletion Request

Email us from the address on your account, with the subject “Account Deletion Request”. We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

getadaapp@gmail.com

What is deleted

Your account and the personal data linked to it: your sign-in details, your profile (names, date of birth, nationality, phone number, region, resident status, university details, language), your profile picture, your push notification token, and the reviews, questions, answers and messages you posted.

What is not

Counts that were never linked to you — for example how many people tapped a business’s call button — are kept, because they contain nothing that identifies you. The Privacy Policy describes every kind of data we hold and how long we keep it.

Remove some data without deleting your account

In the app you can delete your own reviews, questions and messages, clear your phone number, and turn off your listing in a university’s student list. For anything else, email getadaapp@gmail.com and tell us what you would like removed; we reply within 30 days.
